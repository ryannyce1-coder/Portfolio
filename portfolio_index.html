Fire Displaces Boston Family After Dryer Malfunction

Hard-news report covering a residential house fire in Boston's Oak Tree Heights neighborhood, examining fire alarm safety and community impact.

"I was caught off guard by the fire, but I am thankful we made it out safely," James Henderson said.

A house fire caused by a dryer malfunction displaced the Henderson family — James, Jennie, and 15-year-old Charlie — early Monday at 4:55 a.m. Local firefighters arrived at 5:07 a.m. and extinguished the fire in approximately two hours.

All five smoke alarms in the home were found to be non-functional. Fire Chief Rick Ramirez confirmed the cause and noted that the fire department offers free Saturday safety classes. A GoFundMe has been established for the displaced family.

The incident is one of 10 fires in the Boston area over the past six months; seven involved no alarms or faulty ones, resulting in three deaths and six major injuries.

Smoke Out Inc. to Host Fire Safety Event After Oak Tree Fires

A public relations news release drafted on behalf of Smoke Out Inc., promoting a community fire safety event in response to a string of local fires.

For Immediate Release — April 27, 2026

BOSTON — Smoke Out Inc. will host a fire safety event on May 15 from 6–9 p.m. at Oak Tree Elementary School, following the recent fires in the Boston neighborhood.

The event will provide free smoke detectors for the first 50 attendees (one per family). Additional smoke detectors will be available for purchase.

"Smoke detectors save lives. Period. We live in this community too, and it breaks my heart that neighbors have lost loved ones, homes, and precious possessions unnecessarily." — Lee Williams, President

The event schedule includes fire safety presentations (6–6:30 p.m.), fire escape route planning (7–7:30 p.m.), family and home protection (8–8:30 p.m.), and a guest speaker at 8:30 p.m. A silent auction, firetruck viewing, and meet-and-greet with Sparky the Fire Dog and Smokey Bear are also planned.
